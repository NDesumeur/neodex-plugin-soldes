# Sales

Prices of the products you are waiting for, watched for you.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

- Paste a **product page** link and an optional **target price**. NeoDex reads the price from the page and checks again **every 6 hours**.
- A **notification** tells you when it drops. Each product has its price chart, lowest price and change since the start.
- Some sites block robots: type the price **by hand** and the chart goes on.

## Permissions

- **Storage**: your products and price history. **Network (all sites)**: read only the product pages you add. **Notifications**, **Background**: check every 6 hours.
