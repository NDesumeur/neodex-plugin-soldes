# Sales

Prices of the products you are waiting for, watched for you.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

- Paste a **product page** link and an optional **target price**. NeoDex reads the price from the page and checks again **every 6 hours**.
- A **notification** tells you when it drops. Each product has its price chart, lowest price and change since the start.
- Some sites block robots: type the price **by hand** and the chart goes on.

## Watch this price (new in 1.1.0)

On a product page opened in NeoDex, the WATCH THIS PRICE button of the address bar tracks it in one click, with the price shown. On a product already tracked, it notes the current price.

## Permissions

- **Storage**: your products and price history. **Network (all sites)**: read only the product pages you add. **Notifications**, **Background**: check every 6 hours.

## Watch this price, from the browser (1.1.0)

On a product page opened in NeoDex, a **WATCH THIS PRICE** button appears right of the address bar. One click, and the product is tracked with the price shown, without leaving the page.

- The button only appears on product pages (those that give their price to search engines, like most shops).
- The price is read **in the page you see**: it also works on sites that block robots.
- On a product already tracked, the same button **notes the current price** and tells you how much it moved.
- Never in a private tab. Sales only sees the address, the title and the product data, and only when you click.

NeoDex 3.4.2 or newer is needed.
